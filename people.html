Ahmed Khan

PhD Student

What is the relationship between agenthood and selfhood? I am interested in studying this question using the formalism of reinforcement learning: To understand why and when it is useful for artificial, reward-maximising agents to reason about and refer to their self, and how they might do so in practice. This research draws on existing directions in computational neuroscience and philosophy, that similarly examine the structure and salience of the self in natural intelligence.
